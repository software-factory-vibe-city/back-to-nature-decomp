# Retro: ovl_10_func_800BA394 — the two-stage hoist cascade, and the loop the pass refused to see

Final state: byte-identical (479/479, `make check-all` green) with plain C89
under baseline flags. No override, no shield, no pinning. Seven sessions in
total; the first six closed axes, the last one found the mechanism. The full
measured history is in the experiment ledger; the mechanism record is in the
closed-directions log. This retro is the compressed why.

## The residual

Two words at block 93 for six sessions: the row loop's preheader wanted
`[row=0][la header][off=0][la payload]` and every clean-C spelling produced
`[row=0][la header][la payload][off=0]`. The preheader layout law —
`[source][p1 movables][p1 giv inits][p2 movables][p2 giv inits]` — forces the
payload materialisation to be a *pass-2* emission, and sessions 2–6 proved,
correctly, that no walking-pointer or single-index spelling can decline
pass 1: the pointer form is a forced %hi/%lo pair whose desirability product
floors at 4 against a bar of 3, and the index form folds its %lo into the load
and lands in the wrong preheader.

## What was wrong with the frame

Both regimes placed the payload materialisation at the *row* level, because
the target's body walks a pointer and a walker needs a row-level init. The
target reaches its shape through neither regime:

1. **The payload address lives per-iteration in the INNER loop**, spelled as a
   two-register-sum index (`D_800BB9BC[s7 * 0x10 + s2]`). Two registers in the
   index mean the %lo cannot fold into the load, so the pair stays standalone
   — inside the inner loop, where the sweep never put it.
2. **Pass-1-inner hoists the pair** into the row body (its preheader).
   Insns created by a loop pass are skipped by the enclosing scan — pass-1-row
   never re-evaluates them — and pass-2-row then re-hoists them to block 93,
   *after* pass 1's giv init. The sibling `ovl_10_func_800B95F0`'s trace had
   demonstrated this cascade all along (`Insn 1021 moved to 1058`).
3. **The walker is not source.** The address giv (mult 1, compound add
   `off + base`) carries benefit 8, clears the reduce gate, and becomes the
   walking pointer; its init is the target's `addu s1, s3, fp`.
4. **The index is not source either.** The target's `move s0, s3` sits *after*
   the walker init — impossible for a source insn, since strength-reduce
   appends at the loop-start note. Spelling both the selection test and the
   inner tail as `s7 * 0x10 + s2` makes two identical givs whose benefits
   combine (4 + 4), clearing the gate, and the reduced giv *is* `s0`.

## The trap that cost two of the failures

The `&&`-form inner tail (`while (s2 < 0x10 && idx < count)`) laid the loop
out with an entry jump, and gcse's PRE insertions then landed between
`NOTE_INSN_LOOP_BEG` and the entry jump — making `scan_start` a plain insn, so
`loop.c:740` discarded the loop as *phony* and no inner scan ever ran. The
break-form tail (`s2++; if (s2 >= 0x10) break; } while (idx < count)`) keeps
the fallthrough entry, the insertions land before the note, and the loop is
scanned. A phony inner loop silently converts the whole cascade into the
already-refuted row-level regime; check the trace for `phony` before reasoning
about any per-iteration placement.

## Transfer

with the mirror-image defect: its payload cascade already works (its attempt
