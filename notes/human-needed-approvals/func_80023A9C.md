## Resolution (2026-08-19)

The parked loop promised that the target's count-up `sltiu+bnez` walking-store
loop was "only reachable with `-fno-strength-reduce`" and that every spelling
of the loop compiled to the count-DOWN `bgez` form. That claim was falsified
empirically: the **period-idiom naive indexed loop** `D_800A0708[i] = 0xFFD`
strength-reduces into exactly the target's count-up walking form under the
baseline `-O2 -G8` flags — no override needed. (Same lesson as
`notes/research/func_8001FF98-gcse-pre-blocks-loop-strength-reduction.md`:
period source writes naive indexed loops and lets strength reduction manufacture
the walkers.)

The other half of the residual (an extra `move s0,v0` local-alloc carrier on a
gcse reaching-reg temp) also came from the transcribed pointer-walk source:
writing `u16 *p = &D_800A0708[0]; *p++ = keep` creates a bb0-local `high`
quantity that local-alloc puts in `$v0`, while the naive indexed form keeps the
`high` on the single call-crossing `$s0` web. `psx_solve_local_allocation`
returned UNSAT for the pointer form (no priority/phantom change yields
85→$s0), and the instrumented compiler proved forcing 85→$s0 makes the pointer
form byte-exact — but the naive indexed form reaches it naturally with no
force.
